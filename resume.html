How I work

  • When production data looks wrong, I check the source records and trace the flow before changing code.
  • I keep uncertain identity matches out of automatic merges and send them to review instead.
  • I try to make sync and import jobs safe to run again after a partial failure.
  • I prefer the existing framework and a small change over adding another service or library without a clear need.
  • For AI features, model output is checked before it can affect business data or user-facing limits.
