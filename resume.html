Engineering approach

  • Evidence before fixes: reproduce, trace authoritative data, quantify impact, then change the smallest necessary boundary.
  • Correctness over convenient guesses: keep ambiguity explicit, validate external data, and prefer safe fallbacks to plausible-but-wrong state.
  • Proportional architecture: add frameworks, services, state libraries, or AI only when the product requirement earns the complexity.
  • Recoverable systems: design writes to be retryable/idempotent, preserve last-known-good state where appropriate, and make failure visible.
  • AI as an interface layer: deterministic business rules remain authoritative; model output is validated, bounded, and replaceable.
